Refund & Cancellation Policy

Because we provide personalized, manual digital services, our refund policies are strictly enforced without exceptions.

Last Updated: July 2026

At NovaplexesResume, our experts invest their personal time and manual effort into drafting, formatting, and optimizing every single resume. Because our product is a personalized digital service, it cannot be "returned" once created. Therefore, we enforce a Strict Zero-Refund Policy under the following terms.

1. The Zero-Refund Rule

Once the payment has been made and our team has begun processing or working on your details, absolutely NO REFUNDS will be issued.

Refunds will NOT be granted under any of the following circumstances:

  • Change of Mind: You simply decide you no longer want the resume.
  • Found Another Job/Service: You secured a job or hired someone else after placing the order with us.
  • Subjective Dissatisfaction: You do not "like" the design. (We offer a Revision Policy to handle reasonable adjustments, but dissatisfaction is not grounds for a refund).
  • No Interview Calls: We do not guarantee jobs or interviews. A lack of HR response does not entitle you to a refund.

2. Strict Cancellation Window

You may request a cancellation only within 2 hours of placing the order AND only if our writers have not yet started working on your document.

  • If these conditions are met, a refund will be processed, minus a flat 20% administrative and payment gateway processing fee.
  • If the 2-hour window has passed, or if work has already commenced, the cancellation request will be automatically denied.

3. Client Non-Responsiveness (Forfeiture of Funds)

To craft your resume, we require your work history and details. If you place an order but fail to provide the required information via WhatsApp or Email within 7 Days of your payment, your order will be permanently closed.

In such cases of client negligence or non-responsiveness, your payment will be 100% forfeited. No refunds will be provided, and reopening the project will require a completely new payment.

4. Revisions Instead of Refunds

We are committed to delivering a quality product. If there are minor errors or formatting issues, you must utilize your designated revision rounds (as stated in your Basic or Premium plan). Requesting a revision is your sole remedy. Demanding a refund in lieu of a revision is strictly prohibited.

5. Unjustified Chargebacks and Disputes

If you falsely file a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or UPI provider after we have delivered the digital service, we will consider it an act of fraud.

  • We will immediately submit all communication logs, WhatsApp chats, and delivery proofs to the payment gateway and relevant authorities.
  • Any legal disputes arising from fraudulent chargebacks will be handled strictly within the jurisdiction of the courts located in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India.
